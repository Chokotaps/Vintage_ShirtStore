About Us

Welcome to Rai's Vintage T-shirt Store, where vintage fashion meets modern style! We believe that every piece of clothing has a story to tell, and we’re here to help you find the one that speaks to you.


What We Offer

Our collection features handpicked vintage gems from different eras, including:

  • Retro 70s boho-chic styles
  • 80s statement pieces
  • streetwear & grunge classics
  • Rare designer finds

Every item is carefully inspected to ensure quality, so you can wear your vintage pieces with confidence.


Why Vintage?

Shopping vintage isn’t just about style—it’s about making a statement. By choosing pre-loved clothing, you're reducing waste, supporting sustainable fashion, and embracing pieces that have stood the test of time.
